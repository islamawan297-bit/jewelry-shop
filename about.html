Our Heritage

The Dream

Molded from Fire & Gold

AURUM was founded in 1998 with a singular, clear ambition: to create fine jewelry that bridges the gap between classic antiquity and modern, sleek design. What began as a small boutique workshop of three passionate gold smiths in Milan has evolved into a global luxury brand, carrying the spirit of master Italian craftsmanship to jewelry collectors worldwide.

We believe that jewelry is more than just personal adornment; it is a repository of memories, a symbol of commitment, and an heirloom designed to pass through generations. This philosophy dictates every line sketched and every stone cut in our private studios.

Every piece in our collection is crafted with the highest standards of materials, utilizing 18K solid yellow, rose, and white gold, alongside ethically sourced diamonds of exceptional clarity (VS1-VS2 or better).

Artistry in Details

Exquisite Artisanal Process

Our craftsmanship process is slow, deliberate, and detailed. We reject the standards of mass production, choosing instead to focus on individual creation.

1. Hand Sketching: Our design directors sketch every collection by hand, focusing on form, light play, and ergonomics to ensure rings rest comfortably and pendants drape beautifully.

2. Stone Selection: Our certified gemologists inspect thousands of loose stones, hand-selecting diamonds and sapphires that match perfectly in color matching, cut angles, and luster.

3. Micro-Setting: In our setting rooms, master setters work under micro-magnification to securely claw-set and pave diamonds, maximizing light entry for unmatched sparkle.

4. High Polishing: Each piece undergoes a multi-stage buffing and hand-polishing process, achieving a flawless, mirror-like finish that catches even the lowest ambient light.

Our Commitments

Corporate Values

100% Ethical Sourcing

We adhere strictly to the Kimberley Process, sourcing conflict-free diamonds and recycled gold to reduce ecological impact while protecting mining communities.

Uncompromising Quality

We only employ 18-karat solid gold and diamonds of G-H color and VS clarity or better, offering certified authentication details with every purchase.

Luxury Signature Box

Every creation is packaged in our signature heavy velvet-lined mahogany wood presentation box, making unboxing a timeless ceremony in itself.
